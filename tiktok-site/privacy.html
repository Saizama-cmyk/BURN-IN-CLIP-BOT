Privacy Policy

ClipBot runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It has no servers and collects no data from anyone else.

  • What it receives from TikTok: an access token, a refresh token and your TikTok open ID after you approve the connection, plus your account's allowed privacy settings, which it needs before posting.
  • Where it is kept: only in a local file on the owner's PC (%LOCALAPPDATA%\ClipBot\tokens.json). It is never sent anywhere except back to TikTok's own API.
  • What it is used for: uploading videos you approved to your own TikTok account. Nothing else.
  • Sharing: none. No analytics, no ads, no third parties.
  • Deletion: revoke access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services) and uninstall ClipBot, or delete the tokens file.
